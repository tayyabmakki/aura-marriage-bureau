## 3. FAQ (original answers; confirm every factual claim)

**Is my information private?**
Yes. Names, father's names, addresses, contact numbers, employer details and photographs stay confidential. Basic details and general requirements are shared with other registered families only for matchmaking, and only with your permission.

**Do you verify families?**
We pass on information exactly as each family gives it to us. We are a facilitator, not an investigator, so every family should do its own checks before making any commitment.

**Do you handle overseas proposals?**
Yes. We work with families in Pakistan and with overseas Pakistanis in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, UAE and elsewhere.

**Do you handle second marriages?**
Yes. Divorced, khula and widowed clients are welcome and are treated with the same dignity and care as everyone else.

**Can a consultant attend the family meeting?**
Yes, if either family asks. Additional charges apply to both families in that case.

**Who can register?**
Parents, legal guardians or elder siblings. We do not accept proposals from minors.

**Do I have to fill the form on this website?**
No. The form only helps you prepare your details neatly. You copy it and send it to us on WhatsApp yourself, or you can simply share your details in a WhatsApp message.
